# Scout notes: peak-bagging / list completion

## Candidate: Peak-list progress board / finisher card (NH48, ADK 46, CO 14ers)
- Construct: a fixed list of N peaks (48 / 46 / 58), each with a date climbed (plus an optional comment such as companions). Picture = N-cell grid (name, elevation, date; filled or empty) + "31/48" header, or a finisher card when N/N.
- Artifact: the AMC 4000 Footer Club application, a PDF/Excel list of peaks with a date climbed per peak. White Mountain 4000ft Tracker exports CSV "directly into official 4000 Footer Club application". Peakbagger.com has no native CSV export that I could confirm (peakbagger-cli exists, not opened).
- Evidence (opened):
  - https://amc4000footer.org/how-to-apply.html : "please note the dates of your ascents as you make them... you will be asked to fill in the dates on the application"; applications "available in PDF or Microsoft Excel format".
  - https://vftt.org/threads/peakbagger-spreadsheet.44642/post-373258 : hand-made Excel macro sheet to "track your progress on multiple peakbagging lists"; ascent date gets marked on the NH48 sheet; another poster: "I thought the excel spreadsheet that I have going myself... was crazy detailed"; "I want to post some of my stats to my blog". Another: listsofjohn output "strictly in numbers not neat pie charts". (2012)
  - https://apps.apple.com/us/app/white-mountain-4000ft-tracker/id6476589208 : in-app progress bar plus map; CSV export for the application.
  - https://apps.apple.com/app/id6782580168 (Cairn): in-app progress bar and charts; "No social feeds, no photo albums". No share image mentioned.
  - https://14ers.com/mobileapp-faq : site has "14er User Checklists" and "Checklist Statistics"; no share image mentioned on the page I read.
- Served by: apps show progress inside the app; none I read makes a shareable N-cell image. Not verified: Peakbagger.com and Walkhighlands (the forum gave 403).
- Risks: no direct evidence of anyone asking for a shareable graphic; the demand is inferred from hand-made spreadsheets and blog stats. Munros/Wainwrights have Gaelic names with accents (non-ASCII) and 214-282 cells, so stick to the 46/48/58 lists. Not a "recurring" picture unless updated per hike.
- Strength: 5/10

## Rejected
- Munro/Wainwright bagging map: Walkhighlands already maps progress; Gaelic non-ASCII names; 282 cells.
- Ballpark chasing / national park passport: not researched in this budget; parks overlap with existing map apps (same dev makes an "America the Beautiful Pass Map").
