## Assessment
- The NaNo stats graph (cumulative words vs 1,667/day par line to 50k) is replicated by Pacemaker (graph/calendar/table), TrackBear, NaNo 2.0, Reedsy, NovProg (desktop graph), spreadsheets. Sidebar meters: Scribophile meter, WP plugins (Author WIP Progress Bar, Scribometer, NaNo Stats).
- Sharing today = screenshots of the tracker graph. No evidence found (in pages I read) of people hand-making progress graphics or asking for a share-image tool.
- Possible angle: "end-of-month recap card / daily progress clip" from a daily log CSV (date, words) or TrackBear API -> cumulative bars vs par line, 50k goal, streak, best day. Deterministic and batchable, but demand evidence is thin and core picture is served.
- Verdict: weak candidate (3/10).
