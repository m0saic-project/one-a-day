# Scout notes: art-challenge (Inktober / Huevember / Mermay monthly prompt challenges)

## Pages opened
- OK https://www.svslearn.com/news/2026/9/1/the-official-inktober-2026-prompt-list : official 2026 post. Rules: "Make a drawing in ink... Hashtag it with #inktober and #inktober2026... Repeat every day in October! Or, choose a pace that works for you". "31 drawings in 31 days". Prompt list is "a list of different words for each day of the month". The 31 words are shipped as an image, not text (none in page text).
- OK https://blog.iconfactory.com/2024/09/get-eight-legs-up-on-inktober-with-linea-sketch/ : Linea Sketch has shipped free per-day templates "for the sixth year running", "each page clearly labeled by day and prompt". These are drawing canvases (one page per day), not a month compilation.
- OK https://xquissive.com/huevember : Huevember (Matthieu Daures, since 2014): 30 hues, one per day, "prompt list wheel". Creator quote: "The idea was that at the end of the month, when you put all your artworks next to each other in order, you'd notice a sense of continuity through the colours, like some kind of subtle rainbow." and "you'd still have a beautiful gradient of hues at the end of month where you'll be able to put together all the images you made". Community also makes alternative prompt lists (@harlequinsiren, @aztiluth on DeviantArt).
- PARTIAL https://narumynatsue.gumroad.com/l/huevembertemplate : only the title "Huevember Challenge - Blank Template" rendered (JS page). Shows people sell/share blank grids to fill in by hand.
- FAILED https://dic.pixiv.net/en/a/Huevember (403)

## Search-only signals (not evidence)
- Gumroad is full of finished "31 Inktober illustrations" PDF/zine packs; FlippedNormals sells an Inktober Procreate kit with "drawing grids" and a "calendar tracker".
- No search turned up a dedicated "Inktober compilation grid generator". Generic collage makers / Photoshop guide layouts are what people would use.
- Did not find a direct "how do I make my grid" complaint thread in the budget (Reddit blocked).

## Candidate
Monthly challenge compilation grid ("31 cells, day + prompt label, your drawing in each; empty cells for skipped days"), same template doubling as the host prompt-list graphic when no images are given. Huevember variant: per-cell hue swatch from the official wheel, cells ordered so the month reads as a rainbow.
Props: challenge, year, days[{day, prompt, image?, hue?}], columns, accent.
Weaknesses: the prompt list is not a file format (just an image/words), artists are GUI-native (Procreate/Photoshop/Canva), main post format is IG carousel, demand evidence for compilation is mostly implied, generic collage tools cover 80%.

## Rejected
- Host prompt-list graphic alone: hosts make it once a year by hand as branded art; one-off, not batch.
- Per-day labeled drawing canvas: already served free by Linea Sketch templates and Procreate kits.
- Month-end reel of 31 drawings: same as grid, fine as an option but every phone video editor does slideshow.
- Tumblr ship weeks: 7 prompts, very low visual structure, fandom art licensing issues.
